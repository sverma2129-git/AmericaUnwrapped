About this site

The Vietnam War, 1945–1975

In 1945 Ho Chi Minh asked Harry Truman for help. Thirty years later, the last Americans left Saigon by helicopter.

Twelve chapters follow the war through the cables and photographs of the time. We check every quotation word for word against the original, and you can open the documents yourself.
