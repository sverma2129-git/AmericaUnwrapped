About this site

For teachers

The Vietnam War, 1945 to 1975, in 12 chapters with a study guide, for U.S. History and AP U.S. History classes.

It’s free and runs in a web browser. There are no ads, accounts or tracking, so students can open a link and start.

Chapter by chapter

Reading times are estimates for the story text alone, at a careful pace. Allow extra time for the sources, maps and practice questions.

  1. 1. Dien Bien Phu and the end of French rule

    Why was the United States paying for France’s war in Vietnam?

    1945–1954. About 2 minutes of reading, 10 practice questions.

  2. 2. Geneva splits Vietnam in two

    Why were the 1956 elections never held?

    1954–1960. About 2 minutes of reading, 9 practice questions.

  3. 3. Kennedy, Diem and the coup

    Why did the United States help remove the leader it had backed for nine years?

    1961–1963. About 2 minutes of reading, 8 practice questions.

  4. 4. The Gulf of Tonkin

    How did an attack that most likely never happened lead America into war?

    1964. About 2 minutes of reading, 10 practice questions.

  5. 5. America takes over the war

    Why couldn’t half a million American troops wear North Vietnam down?

    1965–1967. About 2 minutes of reading, 9 practice questions.

  6. 6. The Secret War

    What did America’s secret war in Laos and South Vietnam cost?

    1961–1972. About 2 minutes of reading, 9 practice questions.

  7. 7. The Tet Offensive

    Why did a battle Hanoi lost turn Americans against the war?

    1968. About 2 minutes of reading, 9 practice questions.

  8. 8. Nixon and Vietnamization

    Did Nixon believe South Vietnam could fight on its own?

    1969–1972. About 3 minutes of reading, 9 practice questions.

  9. 9. The Paris Peace Accords

    Why didn’t the Paris Peace Accords end the war?

    1972–1973. About 2 minutes of reading, 8 practice questions.

  10. 10. The Fall of Saigon

    How did South Vietnam’s army collapse in just 55 days?

    1973–1975. About 3 minutes of reading, 9 practice questions.

  11. 11. After the war

    What did thirty years of war cost Vietnam?

    1975–1995. About 2 minutes of reading, 8 practice questions.

  12. 12. How historians argue about the war

    Could the United States have won the Vietnam War?

    1975 to today. About 3 minutes of reading, 8 practice questions.

Before you assign it

  • The unit covers war deaths, executions and the shooting of student protesters, and some photographs show the violence of war. Preview the chapters you plan to assign.
  • Nothing is sent to you. To see how a student did, ask them to show you the results after the quiz.
  • The study guide has 106 practice questions. They aren’t your test, so tell students which chapters yours covers.
  • Footnotes name the sources. Check any source you plan to teach from, and see About Us for how the site handles quotations.
