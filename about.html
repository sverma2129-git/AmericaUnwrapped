Charter & Standards

The America Unwrapped Editorial Standard

America Unwrapped should explain the history, not tell the reader what political conclusion to reach.

Our Intellectual Mission

Most historical coverage online falls into two unsatisfactory camps: dry academic databases that overwhelm readers without providing clarity, or partisan commentary that treats history as a weapon for modern culture wars.

America Unwrapped was founded on the belief that American history is most compelling when presented with complete intellectual honesty, generous whitespace, primary source transparency, and an unwavering focus on causation: Decision → Assumption → Result → Next Decision.

The Ten Editorial Rules

RULE 01

Evidence Before Conclusion

We do not begin with a political narrative and backfill evidence to support it. Every interpretation is generated by the contemporaneous archival record.

RULE 02

Equal Intellectual Scrutiny

Washington and Hanoi receive identical critical examination. We scrutinize American bureaucratic hubris and credibility traps with the same rigor applied to North Vietnamese internal purges, ideological dogmatism, and human sacrifice.

RULE 03

Separate Fact from Interpretation

We maintain a clear distinction between verifiable historical events (e.g., the date and naval movements in the Gulf of Tonkin) and the contested interpretations surrounding why they occurred.

RULE 04

No Manufactured False Balance

Where empirical documentation is overwhelming—such as the non-existence of North Vietnamese patrol boats on the night of August 4, 1964—we state the consensus plainly rather than fabricating artificial symmetry.

RULE 05

Show Genuine Disagreements Honestly

When serious historians disagree—such as whether the war was winnable or whether Kennedy planned to withdraw—we explain the conflicting evidence and the rationale of each historiographical school.

RULE 06

Neither Side Was Omniscient

Historical actors acted under extreme uncertainty, fog of war, and incomplete intelligence. We evaluate decisions in light of what leaders actually knew at the time, rather than judging them with retrospective clairvoyance.

RULE 07

No Historical Actors as Cartoons

Leaders, soldiers, and diplomats were complex human beings operating within domestic political constraints, institutional pressures, and ideological worldviews. We reject simplistic caricatures of heroes and villains.

RULE 08

Neutral Language Over Loaded Rhetoric

We use precise, neutral language. Emotional manipulation and moralizing adjectives obscure historical causation; clear prose clarifies it.

RULE 09

Primary Sources Support Major Claims

Key assertions link directly to declassified government cables, treaty texts, intelligence estimates, and Politburo resolutions. Readers can inspect the original words for themselves.

RULE 10

Human Scholarship Over Synthetic Authority

AI is an architectural and engineering tool, never the historical authority. Our content is grounded in peer-reviewed monographs, declassified repositories, and archival source material.

Digital Publication Architecture

In keeping with our documentary and bookish aesthetic, America Unwrapped is built as an ultra-fast, static digital publication:

Zero SurveillanceNo third-party trackers, telemetry, or advertising pixels.
Structured Local DataNo external database or fragile CMS dependencies; all historical data is typed and version-controlled.
Permanent AccessibilityPrerendered static markup ensures complete longevity and sub-second load times.
